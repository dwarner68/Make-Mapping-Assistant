How this was built: Each Old Make value is normalized (trailing lengths, HP, and unit words stripped, common engine-brand shorthand like "MERC" expanded) and matched against a ~840-name canonical list spanning Marine, RV, Trailer, Golf Cart, and Powersports manufacturers, compiled from industry directories. Exact and Strong matches are auto-filled with high confidence; Fuzzy matches catch typos/misspellings and are worth a glance. Everything else is 🚩 flagged for review and defaults to OTHER: Not a make rows (unit numbers, placeholders, generic terms like "BOAT #12", "RENTAL", "PONTOON") were never a manufacturer name to begin with, and Needs review rows just didn't clear the confidence bar — those show the closest guess (if any) as a hint in case you'd rather use it. Both are marked with a 🚩 and can be isolated with the filter above. Every New Make value — suggested or typed — is saved in ALL CAPS. Edit any cell directly before exporting; nothing is uploaded anywhere, it all runs in your browser.
